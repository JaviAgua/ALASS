ALASS: Assessment of Language in Adults using Self-reported Skills

Revised 31-item version · free to use under CC BY 4.0

Your answers stay on this device. Scores are calculated in your browser; nothing is saved or sent anywhere. The ALASS is a research and screening questionnaire, not a diagnostic test. It has no clinical cut-off scores. If you are concerned about your language, please speak to a speech and language therapist.

Each score is the mean of the items you answered; higher scores mean better self-reported skills. For comparison only, a sample of 130 UK university students had means of CL 8.10, LP 8.56, SUL 7.39 and total 8.22. These describe one student sample and are not norms or cut-offs.

Scoring key. Childhood Language (CL): A1–A13. Language Performance (LP): B1, B2, B4, B8–B18. Social Use of Language (SUL): B3, B5, B6, B7. Total: all 31 items. No item is reverse-scored.
